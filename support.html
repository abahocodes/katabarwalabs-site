Before you write in

  • Our apps run in your own environment. They deploy into your Azure subscription, AWS account, or Atlassian/SaaS instance and run under an identity you grant.
  • Nothing leaves your tenant. Alerts and reports are delivered only to the Microsoft Teams, Slack, or email endpoint you configured during setup — check that first if you’re not receiving them.
  • Most products ship read-only / dry-run by default. Anything that changes or deletes must be explicitly enabled after you review a report.

Common setup notes

  • Each product includes a one-time post-deploy step to grant its identity the least-privilege role it needs — see the product’s listing page for the exact command.
  • Teams delivery uses a Power Automate “Workflows” webhook (the classic Office 365 connector was retired); paste that webhook URL into the app’s configuration.
  • If a scheduled run is missed, most products self-heal on the next run.

Billing

Subscriptions are billed by the marketplace you purchased on (Microsoft, AWS, or Atlassian). For invoice or payment questions, use your marketplace account; for anything product-related, email us directly.
