One idea, applied across every enterprise marketplace: the tool runs where your data already lives, and the marketplace handles billing. We operate no servers, so “nothing leaves your tenant” is literally true — which is exactly what your security review wants to hear.

Runs in your environment

A serverless function or managed package deploys into your own subscription, account, or instance under an identity you control. Read-only by default.

No vendor backend

We host nothing and receive none of your operational data. Alerts go only to the Slack, Teams, or email endpoint you configure. Remove the app and every trace is gone.

Flat, predictable pricing

The marketplace bills a flat monthly price and pays us — no metering surprises, no invoicing, no separate contract.

What we cover

The same handful of pains recur on every platform. We build them once and port the shape:

  • Audit-log retention & export — keep evidence past the platform’s short retention window.
  • Orphaned-owner & stale-identity cleanup — reclaim resources and seats left by people who left.
  • Access governance — who can do what, permission drift, audit-ready reports.
  • Credential, secret & certificate hygiene — alert before things expire and break production.
  • Cost, waste & coverage gaps — find leaks, orphaned resources, and unprotected assets.
